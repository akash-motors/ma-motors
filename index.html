About Us

MA Motors is an automotive company founded in 2025 with a vision to revolutionize the Indian automobile industry by introducing high-performance electric vehicles and smart mobility solutions. Our journey began with the belief that the future of mobility must be sustainable, innovative, and accessible.

MA Motors embodies the company's mission to transform the automobile driving and ownership experience—from mechanical to automated—through cutting-edge technology, innovative design, and a foundation of trust and togetherness.

At MA Motors, innovation, efficiency, and a customer-first approach are at the heart of everything we do. Officially established in 2025, Our vision has been evolving in our minds over the years, built on decades of experience, insight, and ambition. We are entering the automobile industry with a bold mission: to design and manufacture next-generation vehicles that merge advanced technology, environmental responsibility, and exceptional performance.

Our vehicles are thoughtfully engineered to challenge and disrupt the conventional market landscape, traditionally dominated by a select few. The journey from concept to creation has been deliberate and research-driven. As a team of seasoned professionals, we value thorough analysis, continuous refinement, and strategic planning before execution — ensuring that every decision reflects the best possible approach.

Investment and Infrastructure

Initially we have planned to induce INR 100 Cr for the initial set up and operational cost of the company. The production setup will be as per the international automotive norms and requirement to maintain the automotive standards which will include manufacturing, testing, validation and inventory storage.

As part of our long-term vision, we are also planning to set up a Gigafactory focused on the production of petrol and electric two-wheelers, EV batteries, and an integrated battery swapping system. This facility will play a key role in building a future-ready, scalable infrastructure to support India's growing demand for clean mobility solutions.

Dome shape warehouse structures shortlisted for plant:

  • Dharuhera - Haryana: 15 acres, built-up 4 lac sq ft
  • Palwal - Haryana: 20 acres, built-up 6 lac sq ft
