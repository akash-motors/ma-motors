About Us

MA Motors is an automotive company founded in 2022 with a vision to revolutionize the Indian automobile industry by introducing high-performance electric vehicles and smart mobility solutions. Our journey began with the belief that the future of mobility must be sustainable, innovative, and accessible.

Headquartered in New Delhi and with an operational office in Pune, MA Motors is committed to building cutting-edge electric scooters and motorcycles tailored to Indian roads and consumer preferences. Our leadership team is composed of industry veterans and engineers with global exposure and deep experience in product development, automotive design, and renewable technologies.

At MA Motors, we strive to balance affordability with futuristic design and functionality. We believe in creating vehicles that not only meet performance expectations but also contribute toward a cleaner, smarter environment.

Investment and Infrastructure

Initially we have planned to induce INR 100 Cr for the initial set up and operational cost of the company. The production setup will be as per the international automotive norms and requirement to maintain the automotive standards which will include manufacturing, testing, validation and inventory storage.

As part of our long-term vision, we are also planning to set up a Gigafactory focused on the production of petrol and electric two-wheelers, EV batteries, and an integrated battery swapping system. This facility will play a key role in building a future-ready, scalable infrastructure to support India's growing demand for clean mobility solutions.
